ANSI S3.5-1997 / ISO 16832 Calculation Architecture (v2.0.0 Metrology)

1. Spectrum Level Normalization

ANSI S3.5 equations require all inputs in Spectrum Level ($dB\text{ re }20\,\mu Pa^2/Hz$). The analyzer normalizes integrated band power using exact $1/3$-octave bandwidths ($\Delta f_i$):

Lspectrum, i = Lband, i - 10·log10(Δfi)

Eliminates artificial high-frequency inflation ($+15.7\text{ dB}$ at $160\text{ Hz}$ to $+32.7\text{ dB}$ at $8\text{ kHz}$).

2. Upward Spread of Masking (Vi)

High-intensity low-frequency noise masks higher frequencies through basilar membrane mechanics:

Ci = 0.6·[Bi-1 + 10·log10(fi-1)] - 6.35
Vi = Bi-1 - 3.32·Ci·log10(fi / fi-1)

Effective disturbance $D_i = \max(N_i, X_i, V_i)$ properly models HVAC and road rumble suppression.

3. Decoupled Continuous Metrology

DSP updates are time-normalized via continuous exponential decay constants:

α = 1 - e-Δt / τ   (τ = 0.25 s)

Guarantees identical, jitter-free smoothing and dynamic tracking across $60\text{ Hz}$, $120\text{ Hz}$, and $144\text{ Hz}$ mobile and desktop screens.
